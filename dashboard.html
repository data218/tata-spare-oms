Dashboard Calculations

This section details the logic and formulas used across the dashboard to generate metrics and insights.

1. Inventory Valuation

  • Total Value (₹): Available Stock × NDP (Net Dealer Price)
  • Total Tax (₹): Calculated from Price List tax rates

2. PPNI (Parts Purchased Not Installed)

  • Condition: Parts with Stock > 0 but lacking recent billing or installation activity.
  • Ageing (Days): Current Date - Part Received Date

3. Reorder Management (ROP)

  • Daily Consumption (ADC): 30-Day Consumption ÷ 30
  • Reorder Point (ROP): ADC × Lead Time (Days) + Safety Stock
  • Recommended Order Qty: Max(0, ROP - Available Stock)

4. Part Claims Analytics

  • Gap in Days: Claim Request Date - Part Received Date
  • Contribution (%): (Category Amount ÷ Total Claims Amount) × 100

5. Lubricants (Lube Qty)

  • Categories: LUBRICANT, LUBRICANTS, LUBE, LUBES, OIL
  • Quantity Conversion: System automatically divides sheet values (ML) by 1000 to display in Liters (L).
